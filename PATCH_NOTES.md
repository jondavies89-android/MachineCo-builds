# MachineCo — build-1 (2026-11-09)
The first playtest build: a neon pachinko machine you grow from one ball. Nothing is saved yet, so every visit starts fresh, and all prices and numbers are first drafts.

## New
- Drop balls onto a pin board; they bounce under real physics, land in one of 7 buckets and pay cash (edge buckets pay most), then ride a belt home to drop again.
- A guided start: you begin with one ball and the Drop button, and each new feature appears with a short "New:" prompt as soon as you've earned it. Locked buttons show what unlocks them.
- Buy more Normal balls (up to 10), a faster return belt, and auto-release so balls drop by themselves.
- Split pins (cut a ball into two halves) and Nudge pins (lean balls left or right): buy them, place them on glowing points, and tap one to flip, remove or open it.
- Ball levels: balls earn XP and skill points for the Normal ball's talent tree.
- Every bucket has its own upgrade tree, paid in cash, ending in a keystone such as Jackpot or Tithe. Fully upgraded buckets let you grow the board to 9 and then 11 buckets.
- Every placed Split and Nudge pin levels up on its own and has its own talent tree.
- Each bucket shows what it pays; floating cash numbers pop up on every landing, coloured white to gold by how good it was, and big hits shake the screen a little.
- A neon look with one menu bar along the bottom: Balls, Buckets, Pins, Prestige (coming later) and Settings.
- A debug panel under Settings to tweak physics and hand out cash, pins, levels and points.
